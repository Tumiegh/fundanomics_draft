About Fundanomics

Committed to using education as a tool to shape future citizens who will safeguard democratic gains, value voting, and become proactive members of society.

Our Vision

To use education and literature to shape minds today who will lead tomorrow.

Our Mission

To create educational tools and programs that orient the next generation of patriotic and responsible citizens.

Our Purpose

To ensure that as many children as possible have access to quality education programs and tools, igniting reading and learning for meaning.

Statistics on SA Literacy

Basic literacy among children in South Africa has declined according to the 2030 Reading Panel report

Most children leave grade one without knowing the alphabet, while 82% of grade 4 children cannot read with meaning

Fewer primary school children can read for meaning now than before the Covid pandemic

Meaning becomes familiar when children can resonate with their environment and daily experiences

Our Projects

Making a difference in communities across South Africa through education and literacy programs.

Project Pilot - Gauteng Province

The Fundanomics project team has identified Gauteng Province as a pilot area for the distribution of the Democracy Pack. Our goal is to integrate the learning process with the benefits of understanding democracy.

Bright Book Bees

Our literacy intervention program where we collect and distribute books for children in Gauteng communities. We also host Reading Fridays at local libraries and community centers.

Public libraries serve as crucial safe spaces with technological access, providing children with opportunities to read, engage, and interact with their peers in disadvantaged communities.
